A CLASSIC, BY THE RULES

Know your maze.

Eat all 244 dots and power pellets. Avoid the ghosts until a power pellet turns them blue. Clear the board, keep your lives, and go again.

Blinky THE SHADOW

Always on your tail. Gets faster as the dots disappear.

Pinky THE AMBUSHER

Aims ahead of you. Think about where you’re going.

Inky THE WILD CARD

Uses your position and Blinky’s to set a trap.

Clyde THE WANDERER

Chases from a distance. Retreats when you’re close.

3 lives. One extra at 10,000. The dots you’ve eaten stay gone when you lose a life.

Two players, taking turns. Each player keeps their own score, maze and lives.

Arrows / WASD · P · M · F / V Move, pause, toggle sound, and change fullscreen or large view. Esc leaves an expanded view first. Swipe or use the D-pad on touch screens.

A faithful tribute, with limits. Original maze and researched rules; modern movement interpolation, new artwork and synthesized audio. See the repository’s fidelity notes for known differences.
